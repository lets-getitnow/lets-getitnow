**Featured Projects**

**[Rate My Agency](https://github.com/lets-getitnow/ratemy)**

A trust-sensitive reputation platform for the TikTok agency ecosystem. Rate My Agency lets privately verified creators publish anonymous, structured reviews while protecting their identity and limiting abuse. I designed and built the product end to end, including TikTok OAuth, encrypted sessions, review eligibility controls, deterministic reputation scoring, Cloudflare Workers, D1, deployment verification, and automated testing.

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Cloudflare Workers` · `D1` · `TikTok Login Kit`

**[CustomAI / MyBot](https://github.com/lets-getitnow/customai)**

A multi-tenant AI SaaS that turns a TikTok creator’s content and voice into a paid subscription-based chat experience. The system combines creator verification, content ingestion, persona modeling, tenant-scoped RAG, authenticated chat, asynchronous workflows, subscription entitlements, and Stripe Connect payouts—giving creators a complete path from audience to recurring revenue.

`React` · `TypeScript` · `TikTok` · `AWS Amplify` · `Bedrock` · `Aurora pgvector` · `Step Functions` · `Stripe Connect`

**[AI Go Explain](https://github.com/lets-getitnow/ai_go_explain)**

An ML interpretability project focused on making Go neural networks easier for people to understand and learn from. It builds analysis pipelines around KataGo to collect positions, extract internal activations, discover recurring concepts with non-negative matrix factorization, test spatial versus contextual behavior, and generate visual reports for inspecting learned features.

`Python` · `PyTorch` · `KataGo` · `NMF` · `Neural Network Interpretability`
